Industrial Solutions for a Cleaner and More Efficient Future

Advancing Industrial Performance and Sustainability
#

At Tech Seed Enterprise, our mission is to deliver versatile industrial equipment and solutions that drive productivity and create value across a wide range of industries. We are committed to fostering a cleaner, healthier world through innovative products and sustainable practices.

Our Commitment and Core Values
#

“Meet Your Need” is not just a slogan—it is the foundation of our approach. For over 30 years, we have provided cross-industry product services and technical solutions, integrating advanced technologies and professional service teams to address the evolving needs of our customers.

Our expertise covers:

  • Industrial adhesives
  • Specialty chemicals
  • Filtration systems
  • Dust control equipment
  • Product marking solutions
  • Fire-resistant materials
  • Related installation and support services

We continuously introduce advanced technologies from around the world, such as Japan’s CHIKO® dust collectors and HOYA® UV curing systems, successfully applying them in both domestic and international markets. Our focus on product diversity and technical service excellence enables us to offer cost-effective, efficient, and comprehensive solutions that help our clients maintain a competitive edge.

Product Highlights
#

  • Industrial Adhesives: UV glue, hot melt glue, PUR, super glue, anaerobic glue, solvent or water-based glue, AB glue, and more.
  • Glue Applicators: UV curing systems, glue dispensers, hot melt glue applicators, glue guns, conveyor systems, and more.
  • Chemicals & Materials: Conductive adhesives, thermal pastes, industrial putty, firestop materials, mold release agents, industrial cleaners, and more.
  • Filtration System: Filter cartridges, filter bags, filter housings, pumps, oil filters, air filters, and backwash systems.
  • Dust Collection Equipment: Fine dust processing machines, compact dust collectors, non-contact dust collection systems, pneumatic air guns, silent dust collectors, and more.
  • Automatic Marking System: Laser marking and engraving systems, pin marking machines, inkjet printing systems, traceability and auto-production system planning.
