Legacy and Innovation in Industrial Solutions
#

Tech Seed Enterprise Co., Ltd., established in 1994, has built its reputation on steady growth and a commitment to innovative service. The company is dedicated to providing high-quality industrial materials and equipment, serving a wide range of industries with professionalism and expertise.

Evolution and Expansion
#

Initially, Tech Seed focused on adhesives and specialty chemicals. Over time, the company broadened its product portfolio to include industrial filter elements such as filter cartridges, filter bags, and filter housings. This expansion enabled Tech Seed to offer clients a more comprehensive suite of technical solutions tailored to their needs.

Sales and Growth Milestones
#

By 1998, Tech Seed had introduced a line of marking equipment, including automatic pin-marking systems, inkjet printers, and laser marking and engraving machines. This period also marked the beginning of exporting its established adhesive and filtration products to international markets.

To support its growing product range, Tech Seed established five key sales departments:

  • Adhesion
  • Filtration
  • Machinery
  • Chemical Materials
  • Export

Strategic Partnerships and Key Developments
#

  • 2003: Formed a partnership with Filtteck, then Taiwan’s only manufacturer of melt-blown and pleated filter cartridges, strengthening the filtration product line.
  • 2009: Became the official distributor for Japan’s Chiko Airtec Co., introducing compact, high-performance dust collectors for industrial environments.
  • 2014: Entered a marketing partnership with HOYA Corporation (Japan) to promote a full range of UV curing equipment in Taiwan.
  • 2016: Launched the Chemical Materials Department, expanding offerings to include fire-stop materials, industrial putty, mold release agents, and industrial cleaners, further broadening the company’s technical capabilities.

Guiding Philosophy
#

With over three decades of experience, Tech Seed operates with a focus on professionalism, integrity, efficiency, and innovation. Supported by dedicated R&D and service teams in each department, the company delivers high-performance, cost-effective industrial solutions to clients across a diverse array of sectors.
